# Golden Eval Set — Build Rules (Semantic & Conversational Edition)

Generated by `src/rag_sn_in/eval/build_golden_eval.py` from the chunks in
`data/processed/chunks/*.jsonl`. One file per document year, with ≥500 golden
(answerable-only) questions per file. This edition is designed to test
semantic retrieval and conversational robustness—not just exact lexical
matching—using messy, paraphrased, indirect, and non-lexical queries.

## 1. Input filtering (usable chunks)

A chunk is eligible if:

- `text_token_count >= 70` (skips truncated orphan chunks),
- it is not a pure table/noise chunk (unless it contains a table signal that
  can support a grounded table question),
- its `section_path` yields a section label (directly or via fallback to the
  last non-`CHAPITRE` path part),
- it contains enough coherent prose or structured content to support a
  self-contained answer.

Preserve the source wording for the gold answer, but do not require the
question to reuse the source's terminology. The semantic form of a question
must still be answerable from the selected gold chunk(s).

## 2. Candidate question families

For every usable chunk, several families are attempted. A family only fires
when its content signal is found in the chunk and the intended answer is
supported by the chunk (grounding check). Each family should generate a mix of
clean and conversational formulations where appropriate.

| Family | Trigger in chunk text | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| `definition` | definition-signal sentence (« est défini comme », « désigne »…) | « En clair, ça veut dire quoi, la DANC ? » |
| `process` | procedure signal (« marche à suivre », « doit transmettre »…) | « Je dois faire quoi, et dans quel ordre, pour … ? » |
| `obligation` | « il est obligatoire / interdit / requis »… | « Qu'est-ce que je suis obligé de faire concernant … ? » |
| `deadline` | explicit date / « au plus tard » / « avant le » | « C'est pour quand au plus tard ? » |
| `amount` | « € », « % », « montant », « redevance » | « On parle de combien pour … ? » |
| `condition` | « dans le cas où », « sous condition », « si » | « Dans quelle situation cette règle s'applique-t-elle ? » |
| `actor` | « SNCF Réseau », « le gestionnaire », « l'entreprise ferroviaire »… | « Qui s'en charge concrètement ? » |
| `contact` | email/URL + contact verb (cap 30/file) | « Si j'ai un souci, vers qui me tourner ? » |
| `system` | acronym pattern « le système XXX » | « À quoi sert le dispositif qui gère … ? » |
| `factual` | lead sentence containing the topic | « Qu'est-ce que le document prévoit pour … ? » |
| `factual` (overview) | none — works for every section, even without a “nice” topic | « Je cherche l'essentiel de la partie … : que faut-il retenir ? » |
| `list` | enumeration (« - », « : » + items) | « Quels sont les différents cas ou éléments listés ? » |
| `table` | markdown table rows | « Que compare le tableau, et quelles informations donne-t-il ? » |
| `section` | section number only (no topic needed) | « Tu peux me résumer la section 5.6.6.2 ? » |
| `multi_chunk` | two consecutive same-doc chunks, different section labels, ≤2 pages apart | « En reliant ces deux parties, qu'est-ce que je dois retenir sur A et B ? » |
| `semantic_paraphrase` | any grounded topic with a non-lexical paraphrase | « Si je veux faire X, quelle règle du document me concerne ? » |
| `conversational_followup` | answerable content that naturally supports a follow-up | « Et dans ce cas-là, qui intervient ensuite ? » |
| `messy_query` | grounded content with realistic noise, typo, omission, or disfluency | « c pr qd la date limite pr … ? » |
| `negative_or_contrastive` | explicit exception, prohibition, contrast, or “not applicable” signal | « Qu'est-ce qui n'est pas autorisé, ou ne s'applique pas, ici ? » |

The final four families are semantic stress cases. They must not be generated
by changing facts or inventing an antecedent: the intended interpretation and
gold answer must remain clear to a human evaluator.

## 3. Topic and query-variation rules

- A “nice” topic = section label, 10–80 chars, not generic
  (« introduction », « généralités »…), and not raw numbering.
- Long ALL-CAPS labels are recapitalized for readability.
- Most families require ≥2 topic content-words in the answer
  (the overview family requires ≥1).
- At least 50% of retained questions per file should be semantic or
  conversational variants that do not copy the answer's key noun phrase.
- At least 20% should contain realistic conversational markers, such as
  « en clair », « je cherche », « concrètement », « dans ce cas », or « et si ».
- At least 10% should be messy but interpretable: missing accents, casing or
  punctuation variation, abbreviations, light typos, speech-like fragments,
  or redundant filler. Do not include unintelligible strings.
- Non-lexical questions may use pronouns, descriptions, goals, or functional
  descriptions instead of source terminology, but must retain enough context
  for a human to identify the intended subject.
- Do not add facts, entities, dates, amounts, or conditions that are absent
  from the gold chunk. If a query is intentionally vague, its gold answer
  must still resolve the ambiguity from the section context.
- Keep a balanced mix of direct, indirect, paraphrased, contrastive, and
  follow-up formulations; do not let one template dominate.

## 4. Per-question quality gates

Every candidate is kept only if:

- question ≤ 30 words, ≥ 12 characters (≤22 words remains preferred for
  direct questions),
- the question is understandable and answer-seeking despite any intended
  messiness,
- the question matches no banned pattern (meta-questions, “paragraphe”,
  boilerplate, requests to generate or evaluate text, or unsupported premise),
- gold answer is 50–420 chars and fully grounded: ≥78% of the answer's
  content tokens must appear in the gold chunk (minimum 6 tokens),
- semantic/paraphrase questions have an explicit internal intent mapping to
  the grounded topic; exact lexical overlap is not required,
- conversational follow-ups are independently resolvable from their gold
  chunk(s), or include a deterministic synthetic antecedent in `notes`,
- no extraction artifacts (`[TABLE]`, `[section_header]`) remain in the
  question or answer,
- a messy query contains no ambiguity that would reasonably support multiple
  unrelated gold answers.

For semantic queries, validate both directions: the question's intended
meaning must be supported by the gold, and the gold must directly answer the
question rather than merely mention the topic.

## 5. Deduplication and semantic diversity rules

- Exact dedupe on normalized question text (lowercase, accents/punctuation
  stripped).
- Near-dedupe: Jaccard ≥0.8 on question token sets (year markers ignored) →
  the second one is dropped.
- Semantic near-dedupe: drop a question when it has the same intent, topic,
  and answer span as an earlier question, even if its words differ. Use an
  embedding or intent-similarity check where available; otherwise compare the
  normalized intent frame and grounded answer span.
- At most one question per `(chunk_id, question_type, intent_frame)`.
- Frame diversity: max 3 questions per identical template per file (4 for
  `multi_chunk`); no more than 2 should share the same opening phrase.
- Preserve paraphrase diversity: do not deduplicate solely because a query
  shares a few important domain terms with another query.

## 6. Per-type caps (diversity guard)

`definition` 70 · `process` 85 · `obligation` 60 · `deadline` 65 · `amount` 60 ·
`condition` 65 · `actor` 50 · `contact` 25 · `system` 50 · `factual` 130 ·
`list` 55 · `table` 40 · `section` 60 · `multi_chunk` 70 ·
`semantic_paraphrase` 125 · `conversational_followup` 65 · `messy_query` 55 ·
`negative_or_contrastive` 45.

Selection targets 560 questions/year, stopping at the cap of each type while
maintaining the variation minimums in Section 3. If a variation minimum cannot
be met from the available chunks, record the shortfall in the build log rather
than fabricating examples.

## 7. Cross-year twin and conversational-context rules

A question is eligible for cross-year gold ids only if it is not year-pinned
(the question does not mention the year and the answer contains no year/date/day
reference). For such questions, chunks from the other two years sharing the
same section label and ≥0.25 content overlap are appended to `gold_chunk_ids`
(max 4 ids total, `notes += "; cross-year twins"`). Year-pinned questions keep
same-year golds only.

For `conversational_followup`, the follow-up must not depend on a prior
question that is absent from the row. If a natural-language antecedent is
used, include it in `notes` and ensure the answer remains grounded. Do not
score a follow-up as answerable merely because a neighboring chunk happens to
contain a related term.

## 8. Output schema (exact key order)

```json
{"id", "question", "gold_chunk_ids", "gold_answer", "question_type",
 "difficulty", "requires_year": true, "answerable": true, "notes",
 "collection": "DRR_SNCF", "match_field": "payload.id",
 "document_id", "section_path", "page_start", "page_end"}
```

- `gold_chunk_ids[0]` always belongs to the row's `document_id`.
- `question_type` records the primary family; use `notes` for secondary
  properties such as `semantic`, `conversational`, `messy`, or `non_lexical`.
- `difficulty` should reflect the query form: direct, paraphrased,
  conversational, or messy/indirect—not merely the length of the answer.
- `requires_year` is true only when the question or its answer requires a
  particular document year; otherwise apply the established cross-year rule.
- Generation is deterministic (seeded RNG per document).

## 9. Post-build validation (must all pass)

- ≥500 rows per file,
- schema keys exact; `answerable`/`requires_year` true on every row,
- zero duplicate questions and zero semantic near-duplicates with the same
  intent and answer span,
- zero empty answers/questions; all messy questions remain interpretable,
- every `gold_chunk_ids` exists in the current chunk files,
- every non-`multi_chunk` answer re-passes the 78% grounding check,
- semantic and conversational questions pass human or calibrated semantic
  equivalence review,
- the build reports the Section 3 variation counts and meets them whenever
  enough eligible chunks exist,
- no question's answer depends on unsupported conversational context.

## Operational rule

Chunk ids are positional (`<doc>_chunk_NNNN`). **Any re-chunking renumbers
ids → re-index Qdrant AND regenerate this eval set**, otherwise retrieval
metrics silently compare against stale content (no error, wrong results).

When comparing retrieval systems, report results separately for direct,
semantic/paraphrase, conversational, messy, and contrastive subsets. A single
aggregate score can hide failures on non-lexical queries.
